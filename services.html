Film & TV Services

Protecting Productions. Safeguarding People. Delivering Excellence.

From pre-production through to wrap, Anvil provides comprehensive security, close protection and medical support solutions tailored to the demands of film, television and commercial productions.

Our integrated services are designed to protect cast, crew, high-profile talent and production assets, delivering professional, discreet and dependable support in even the most demanding production environments.

Our services include:

  • Close Protection – Discreet personal security for A-list talent, VIPs, executives and high-profile individuals.
  • On-Set Security – Professional security personnel, location security, access control, crowd management and protection of production equipment and assets.
  • Advanced Medical Cover – Qualified FREC Level 3 and Level 4 responders, alongside experienced paramedics, providing medical support tailored to the specific requirements of each production.
  • Specialist Medical Access – Access to ambulance provision, private doctors and additional medical resources, coordinated to meet production schedules, locations and risk profiles.

At Anvil Global, we understand that every production presents unique challenges. Our bespoke security and medical solutions integrate seamlessly into production operations, allowing cast and crew to focus on what they do best, while we focus on their safety and security.

From intimate location shoots to large-scale productions, we deliver a professional, discreet and reliable service built around the needs of the production.

TSCM

If you have concerns that private conversations or information may be being monitored, our TSCM (technical surveillance countermeasures, or bug sweep) service can check your home, office, vehicle or venue.

With the device owner’s written consent, we can also examine phones, laptops and cameras for signs of tampering or monitoring software. We provide a full comprehensive report. We can advise on next steps, once the client has received and reviewed the final report findings. It can be a one-off, or it can be a regular service.

All our operatives are field trained, and have done foreign and domestic operations. The operatives are discreet and will work around the client.

Surveillance

Need specific information, or need physical confirmation? Then this is the service for you.

We offer all types of surveillance, for companies and legal or investigative clients, and our team can work globally. Operations are both physical and technical. We also offer anti-surveillance, a specialised service with a dedicated team that works to find out who is already surveilling our client.

Every operation is based on the client’s request. Once confidential information is handed over, Anvil assesses and advises on the operation before the client gives the green light.

Our team’s members have experience of surveillance operations in the UK and overseas. The client receives the findings as an encrypted file, with confidentiality covered by the signed NDA, backed by a full report.

How we handle your engagement with us

  1. 01

    Step 1: First contact. When a client first gets in touch, by email or WhatsApp, it is handled personally by either the Anvil team or our Head of Operations.

  2. 02

    Step 2: The call. The first step is a phone call to find out what the proposal is.

  3. 03

    Step 3: NDAs. If Anvil can handle the task, we send NDAs signed by us and by the client. We protect client information using access controls and encryption, and keep hard copies locked away.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Assess and advise. We always assess and advise first, as there may be requirements proposed that aren’t needed, or requirements the client hasn’t considered.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Planning and delivery. Our Head of Operations plans and implements all tasks, with the support of the ground team and the R&D team.

  6. 06

    Step 6: Pre-op brief. For operational purposes, a pre-op brief with a price breakdown is sent to the client for approval.

  7. 07

    Step 7: Updates. This varies from task to task and with the length of the task. If the task is ongoing and the client would like regular updates, this is a predetermined agreement with the client, depending on the length of the task.

  8. 08

    Step 8: Final reports. Any and all finalised reports, as and when required on completion of the task, are shared confidentially with the client on a secure file or drive.
