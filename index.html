Protection Forged In the Heart of the Cotswolds

Anvil was started by a team of professionals from the Cotswolds. Between us we have more than 20 years of experience across the military, close protection, technical surveillance (foreign and domestic) and solving problems for ultra-high-net-worth clients. That covers everything from physical security to the technical work in the background that people never see.

We saw that the world is changing, and that people need our skills more than ever. So we built a one-stop security service. Tell us what you need or what you want, and that is the result you get. Whether it’s your family, your team, your company or your physical assets, if it matters to you, then you’re in the right place.

Think of us as your own personal bow and arrow: you point us in the direction, and we take care of the rest. We work discreetly in the background. Everything is planned by our operations team, overseen by our R&D tech team and carried out by highly trained operatives who are passionate about what they do. Let our answers give you peace of mind.

Get in contact and we will handle the rest.
