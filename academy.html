Training FAQ

Before you book.

Do I need anything before a Close Protection course?

You’ll need an appropriate Level 3 first aid qualification, valid for at least 12 months. Blue and Red ACT Awareness courses must also be completed at least two weeks before attending. We’ll confirm prerequisites during your interview.

What do I need before the Door Supervisor course?

A valid Emergency First Aid at Work certificate is required before the course. Blue and Red ACT Awareness courses must be completed at least two weeks before attending.

Is there an interview?

Yes. Every candidate is interviewed by the Head of Training so we can check the course is suitable and help you prepare.

How many people are on each course?

Courses are limited to eight learners, keeping practical sessions focused and instructor support close at hand.

Is a DBS check needed?

Some courses require a standard DBS check. We’ll explain course-specific requirements and timings during your application.

What time do courses start?

On-site courses at London Oxford Airport start at 08:00. Joining instructions are sent to successful applicants.

Are prices per person? Do you do group bookings?

Prices shown are per person. Group bookings may be available at a discount; contact the Academy to discuss your requirements.

Is funding available?

Contact the Academy and we’ll talk through your circumstances and any funding options that may be available.

Can you train our team on site?

Yes. We can bring selected courses, including first aid and AED training, to your premises. Ask us about group availability.
