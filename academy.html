We’re a local training academy based at London Oxford Airport, training people from across the United Kingdom. If you’re starting out, changing direction or looking after a team, you’re in the right place.

03Courses · Security & Protection

Security & protection courses

SIA Licence Courses

SEC-01 to SEC-04

SEC-01Licence-linked · Level 2

SIA Door Supervisor

Get licensed to work on the doors of pubs, clubs, events and retail. Over six days of practical training you’ll cover the law, conflict management and physical intervention, ready to apply for your SIA Door Supervisor licence. Taught by working operators, not just classroom instructors.

EntryA valid Emergency First Aid at Work certificate is required before the course. Applicants must have completed the Blue and Red ACT Awareness online courses at least 2 weeks before attending the course.

Duration6 days
QualificationQNUK Level 2 Award for Working as a Door Supervisor within the Private Security Industry (RQF)
Price from£235EFAW available separately, £100

SEC-02Refresher · Licence renewal · Level 2

SIA Door Supervisor Refresher

Stay licensed. Stay sharp. For door supervisors renewing their SIA licence. This short, practical course brings your physical intervention skills and door supervision knowledge up to the current standard.

EntryExisting door supervisor licence holders. Valid EFAW required. Applicants must have completed the Blue and Red ACT Awareness online courses at least 2 weeks before attending the course.

Duration2 days
QualificationQNUK Level 2 Award for Door Supervisors in the Private Security Industry (Refresher) (RQF)
Price from£145

SEC-03Licence-linked · Level 3

SIA Close Protection

The full SIA Level 3 close protection qualification, taught by operators who do this work for a living. Eighteen days of route planning, threat assessment, venue security and live exercises. Ideal for service leavers.

EntryAll candidates must complete a FREC 3 medical course before attending. Level 3 first aid with at least 12 months remaining preferred. Applicants must have completed the Blue and Red ACT Awareness online courses at least 2 weeks before attending the course.

Duration18 days (194 hours)
QualificationLevel 3 Certificate for Close Protection Operatives in the Private Security Industry (RQF)
Price from£2,500Or combine with FREC 3

SEC-04Licence renewal

SIA Close Protection Refresher / Top-Up

For close protection operatives renewing their SIA licence. Refreshes safety-critical skills: physical intervention, searches, protecting people in vulnerable situations and terror threat awareness.

EntryExisting close protection licence holders. Level 3 first aid valid for at least 12 months. Applicants must have completed the Blue and Red ACT Awareness online courses at least 2 weeks before attending the course.

Duration3 days
QualificationQNUK Level 2 Award for Close Protection Operatives in the Private Security Industry (Refresher) (RQF)
Price from£350

04Courses · First Aid & Emergency Care

First Aid & Emergency Care

MED-01 to MED-04

MED-01Workplace · Level 3

First Aid at Work

The full workplace first aid qualification that employers need under the Health and Safety (First Aid) Regulations 1981. Covers CPR, AED, bleeding, burns, fractures and medical emergencies. Also meets Level 3 first aid entry for Close Protection.

Duration3 days
QualificationQNUK Level 3 Award in First Aid at Work (RQF)
Price from£350

MED-02Workplace & SIA · Level 3

Emergency First Aid at Work (EFAW)

One practical day that meets the workplace emergency first aid standard and the SIA first aid requirement for door supervisor and security learners. Certificates valid for three years.

Duration1 day
QualificationQNUK Level 3 Award in Emergency First Aid at Work (RQF)
Price from£100

MED-03Teams & venues · Level 3

Defibrillator (AED)

Hands-on day covering CPR and confident AED use. Ideal for teams with an AED on site. We can train on your own device.

GroupsGroup bookings available.

Duration1 day
QualificationQNUK Level 3 Award in Responding to Incidents with an AED (RQF)
Price from£45

MED-04Pre-hospital care · Level 3

FREC 3: First Response Emergency Care

A step up from workplace first aid for people who may be first on scene at serious incidents (event medics, security teams, close protection operatives). Five days of hands-on, scenario-based training.

  • Roles of a first responder
  • Primary survey & vital signs
  • Airway, oxygen & breathing
  • CPR, AED & life-threatening bleeding
  • Trauma, fractures, burns, shock, medical conditions
  • Safe movement, immobilisation & handover
Duration5 days
QualificationQualsafe Level 3 Award in First Response Emergency Care (RQF). Typical validity 3 years
Price from£450

05Featured pathway

One joined-up route into close protection

Complete FREC 3 first, then move straight into Close Protection, so your Level 3 first aid is in place before the Close Protection phase begins.

PTH-01Featured · FREC 3 discount when combined

FREC 3 + Close Protection Combined Pathway

Our combined programme packages both qualifications into one continuous training journey. Ideal if you want to pair advanced first-response medical skills with close protection capability.

  • Complete FREC 3 first, building a strong emergency-care foundation
  • Progress directly into the 18-day Close Protection programme
  • Reinforce medical decision-making within security scenarios
  • One joined-up booking, timetable and learner journey
  1. Step 01 · 5 daysFREC 3

    Emergency-care training and assessment.

  2. Step 02Transition

    Direct progression into the Close Protection phase.

  3. Step 03 · 18 daysClose Protection

    SIA licence-linked training, assessment and practical scenarios.

Duration23 days (5 + 18)
QualificationsQualsafe Level 3 Award in First Response Emergency Care (RQF) and Level 3 Certificate for Close Protection Operatives in the Private Security Industry (RQF)
Price from£2,800Discount applies to the FREC 3 element only when purchased together with the Close Protection course (industry standard).

Why combine?

One booking and one joined-up timetable, with the medical qualification completed before the Close Protection phase.

Who is it for?

New entrants who want a broader skill set for protective security, event work and roles where emergency medical competence matters.

Entry requirement for Close Protection

06Booking, entry requirements & contact

Key information for all courses

  • Limited spaces — 8 seats per course.
  • Interview process to be carried out by the Head of Training.
  • All candidates must complete a FREC 3 medical course before attending a Close Protection course.

  • Discount only applies to the FREC 3 when purchased with the Close Protection course (industry standard).
  • A standard DBS check must be completed 6 weeks prior to all courses.
  • Terms & Conditions apply. All courses are priced per person. Group bookings are available at a discount. For more information please contact info@anvil-global.com
